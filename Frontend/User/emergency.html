Important Government Helpline Numbers

These numbers are provided for important emergency and healthcare-related services.

🚨 Emergency Response

112

India's unified emergency response number for police, fire and medical emergencies.

Call 112

🚑 Ambulance

108

Emergency ambulance service available in many parts of India.

Call 108

🤰 Maternal & Child Health

102

Government ambulance transport service associated with maternal and child health services.

Call 102

👶 Child Emergency Assistance

1098

Child Helpline for children who need immediate assistance or protection.

Call 1098

🆘 Women Helpline

181

Government women helpline number available for assistance and support.

Call 181

🏥 Health Helpline

104

Health-related information and assistance number used by some state health departments.

Call 104
